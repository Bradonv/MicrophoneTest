How to use

  • Hold the phone so the line between its microphones runs left to right, and keep fingers off the microphone holes.
  • Use sharp, broadband sounds: claps, finger snaps, keys, speech. Steady tones above about 1 kHz give false readings.
  • 0° means the sound is square-on to the phone. 90° means it is in line with the two microphones.
  • Two microphones cannot tell front from back, or above from below. Turn the phone and read again to narrow it down.
  • Live listening only works when this page is opened over HTTPS or from localhost.
